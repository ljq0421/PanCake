# 工作台重绘 v3：最终单鏊布局

Use case: style-transfer

Asset type: 16:9 gameplay workstation concept for a Chinese breakfast-stall management game.

References: Use the chosen prior single-griddle concept for camera, countertop depth, one-griddle scale, and side-zone scale. Use the current in-game screenshot as a strict visual-style reference only: friendly cartoon 2D hand-painted forms, clean outlines, flatter cel shading, and readable gameplay silhouettes.

Primary request: Draw the final single-griddle workstation. The game always has exactly one jianbing griddle; there are no griddle upgrades, spare griddle positions, or locked griddle placeholders.

Composition: 16:9 with a shallow, seamless counter along the lower 40–45% of frame and no lower cabinet face. Functional width ratio is exactly 1:2:1: soy-milk at left, jianbing in the center half, youtiao at right. The central tabletop has no visible vertical joints, panel divisions, stripes, or seams.

Stations:

- Left: a compact Chinese insulated soy-milk thermal dispenser barrel with a lid and tap, serving cups, an ice container, and a sugar container. It is a serving/customization station, not a soy-milk-making machine.
- Center: exactly one round jianbing griddle, at the selected reference scale. Immediately right of it is a compact tool group: T-shaped spreader, sauce brush, unlocked sweet-bean and chili sauces, plus later locked sauce containers such as tomato sauce.
- Right: exactly one compact youtiao fryer with its metal draining basket integrated into the machine. No separate brown wicker youtiao basket.

Ingredients: Place small ingredients in individual baskets or crocks along the upper rear counter edge. Use simple lock lids for not-yet-unlocked containers. Never use a bottom ingredient rail, storage-slot strip, or item-button row.

Constraints: No text, logos, watermark, second/third/locked/empty/dotted/ghost griddle, bottom cabinet, extra baskets of youtiao, photorealistic materials, or 3D-render appearance.
