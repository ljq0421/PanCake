Use case: ui-mockup

Asset type: 1920x1080 16:9 gameplay-screen concept art for a Chinese breakfast-stall management game.

Right quarter has a compact youtiao fryer and draining basket. All ingredient supplies sit directly on the rear or side edge of the counter in individual woven baskets, ceramic condiment crocks, or lidded containers. Enabled ingredients are visible; remaining fixed container positions use closed lock lids.

Style/medium: Polished Chinese watercolor-and-gongbi-inspired hand-painted 2D game concept art, tactile food and wood textures, crisp readable silhouettes.

Constraints: Exactly three work zones in 1:2:1 widths; exactly one central griddle; every ingredient in a countertop basket, crock, or container; no lower ingredient rail, bottom storage slots, item-button row, extra appliances, text, logos, or watermark.

Avoid: Photorealism, 3D-render look, cluttered counter, opaque tutorial panels, full-width lower UI tray, and distorted perspective.
