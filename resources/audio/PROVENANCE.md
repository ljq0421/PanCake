# Project Cake breakfast-shop audio provenance

The files in `sfx/` are original deterministic procedural synthesis generated by `tools/generate_kitchen_sfx.py`. They cover pancake cooking, the youtiao fryer, fresh soy milk, packaged drinks, customer service, payment, and a seamless morning ambience. They do not contain third-party recordings, sample-library material, or borrowed melodies.

- Generator: `project-cake-breakfast-shop-audio-v2`
- Format: 32 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM WAV
- Generation date: 2026-08-29
- Rebuild: `python tools/generate_kitchen_sfx.py`
- Byte-for-byte verification: `python tools/generate_kitchen_sfx.py --verify`
- Per-file seeds, duration, gameplay trigger, and SHA-256: `sfx/manifest.json`

This provenance supports commercial-use review but is not a promise of absolute legal zero risk. Final release still requires project-owner legal review and manual listening on target devices.
