Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: project-bound 16:9 game gameplay screen concept art, two-griddle upgrade-stage variant

Primary request: Create an original gameplay concept variant of the referenced late-game Chinese jianbing breakfast stall showing the same three operating stations but only TWO ACTIVE central griddles.

Input images: Image 1 is a strict style, palette, information-hierarchy, and composition reference only; this is a new upgrade-stage variant, not a request to copy characters or text.

Scene/backdrop: warm Chinese neighborhood breakfast stall in the morning.

Subject: left station is the same visibly operating fresh soy-milk machine; center is the dominant jianbing station with exactly two active independent circular griddles, one pancake in the manual spreading stage and one receiving egg and toppings; the third future griddle bay remains visible only as a low-contrast real equipment outline with a single large upgrade-lock icon, completely inactive and with no pancake; right station is the same operating youtiao fryer with oil bubbles and a raised draining basket. Style/medium: polished warm hand-painted 2D game art, slightly cartoonish, tactile food textures, production gameplay mockup.

Composition/framing: exact 16:9 landscape, 1920x1080-safe; compact HUD with large icons and large digits only; left soy, center two active griddles plus one locked future bay, right youtiao; bottom grouped ingredient rail; clear unobstructed work surfaces.

Constraints: exactly three work areas; exactly two active griddles; exactly one locked future griddle silhouette; exactly three customers; preserve the reference's warm visual language and UI hierarchy; no finished packaged-drink cabinet; no steamer; no large instruction/debug panels; no small text; no copied characters; no logos, trademarks, signatures, or watermark.

Avoid: a third active cooking surface, pancakes on the locked bay, photorealism, 3D render look, extra stations, duplicated machines, unreadable decorative text.
